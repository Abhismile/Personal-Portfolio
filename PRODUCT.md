# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

delegated: Vite + React + TypeScript + Framer Motion, chosen for component structure and animation control. Runs locally only for now; deploy target undecided (GitHub Pages or Netlify later).

## Users

Recruiters and hiring managers at FinTech, lending (banks, NBFCs) and accounting firms screening data analyst candidates. They skim fast, often between other applications, and decide in about a minute whether the candidate has real, relevant work.

## Product Purpose

Personal portfolio of Abhishek Shukla, Data Analyst. Success: a recruiter opens a case study within the first minute and comes away believing he can turn messy financial data into trustworthy reports.

## Positioning

A finance-trained analyst (MBA Finance, B.Com) who works on FinTech and accounting data day to day: loans, payments, reconciliation, MIS reporting. The finance background plus hands-on ETL, SQL and Power BI is the claim a generic analyst cannot copy.

## Operating Context

Recruiters arrive from LinkedIn, a job application or a résumé link, on desktop or phone. They compare against other candidates' portfolios, GitHub repos and résumés.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Sections: about, case studies (filterable by tool, open into a full story), experience, skills, certificates, contact.
- All content is edited in `src/data.ts`.
- Primary action: open a case study. Secondary: résumé, email, LinkedIn.

## Evidence on Hand

- Real: two projects (FinTech & Accounting Data Analytics; IPL Cricket Analysis), two roles (D-Table Analytics, Cybrom Technology), PL-300 and IIT Guwahati certificates, stated figures (50,000+ records, 10+ KPIs, 99%+ validation accuracy).
- Absent, must not be fabricated: dashboard screenshots, certificate image files, résumé PDF, location, testimonials, project outcomes beyond what is stated.
- The hero dashboard figure is illustrative and must stay labelled as such.

## Product Principles

1. Proof before claims: show the work and how it was done.
2. Finance fluency is the differentiator; let it show in language and detail.
3. Skimmable in one minute, deep on demand.
4. Never invent numbers, clients or results.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Respect reduced motion; readable contrast in both themes; keyboard-operable dialogs.
